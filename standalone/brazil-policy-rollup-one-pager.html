Core idea: the ontology preserves each paragraph mention as evidence while giving analysts a cleaner rollup view of the policy action and the broader reform episode.

Ontology

Reform episode Broader agenda or process: productivity reform, fiscal reform, pension reform.
Policy action Concrete government move or instrument that can be implemented, delayed, revised, or abandoned.
Paragraph mention One evidence span from the document, with page, paragraph, stance, urgency, and source text.

Aggregation Rule

Do not collapse row-level extractions directly. Preserve every mention as evidence, then assign each mention to a policy action and, when relevant, assign policy actions to a reform episode.

  • Same concrete instrument or government move: same policy_action_id.
  • Different instruments under one agenda: different policy_action_id, same reform_episode_id.
  • Each paragraph mention remains available underneath the rollup as traceable evidence.
